# Conference evidence

Use this evidence when choosing reference papers or explaining why a writing recommendation fits a contribution. Review ratings, selection for a talk, and awards are different signals. Compare scores within the same venue, year, track, and rating snapshot. Verify visible scores with OpenReview where possible and mark archive-only figures. For ICLR 2026, accounts of pre-rebuttal and post-rebuttal scores need care because the conference reset scores to their pre-rebuttal state. [ICLR program chairs' retrospective](https://blog.iclr.cc/2026/03/31/a-retrospective-on-the-iclr-2026-review-process/)

## Corpus and limits

The corpus pass indexed 70,585 proceedings records on 16 November 2026, spanning 18 venue editions. The research-track subset below contains 67,905 records. The additional 2,680 records came from ancillary volumes or sections bundled into the ACL, EMNLP, and AAAI collections. Separately published workshops and the separate NeurIPS Creative AI volume are outside these counts. NeurIPS 2026 proceedings were unavailable at the cutoff, so its three most recent published editions run 2023–2025. The other venues run 2024–2026.

Counts describe proceedings entries rather than announcements of acceptances. NeurIPS counts include research tracks in the main proceedings volume, ACL and EMNLP include main long and short papers plus Findings, and AAAI includes technical and special research tracks. Source records came from proceedings titles and links, the [ACL Anthology XML](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/tree/master/data/xml), and the article tables of 97 [AAAI Press issues](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/issue/archive).

The score screen joined proceedings titles to public rating arrays in the [Paper Copilot OpenReview archive](https://github.com/papercopilot/openreview) by venue, year, and normalized title. It yielded 33,299 matches with at least one usable numeric rating. For sampling, papers with at least three ratings were prioritized and ranked by their mean within the matching venue-year and research track. The examples below report archived numeric ratings rather than live-verified scores, since live OpenReview access was intermittent. Missing score matches include gaps in archive coverage and title matching. ICML 2024 is a snapshot gap despite its [public-review policy](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2024/ReviewerInstructions). Historical scales differ. For ICLR 2026, only one paper appeared on both the high-score and area-chair-nomination lists used to form the award longlist. The selection used further expert input. [Award process](https://blog.iclr.cc/2026/04/23/announcing-the-iclr-2026-outstanding-papers/)

The notes below cover 31 selected papers and their figures, tables, or technical organization. An archive-ranked sample and award citations describe illustrative choices. Estimating preference or an effect on acceptance would require a separately sampled, coded comparison.

## Close reads prioritized by public ratings

### ICLR

- **The Mechanistic Basis of Data Dependence and Abrupt Learning in an In-Context Classification Task** (2024, oral, archived 8/8/10/10). Figure 1 sets the sequence task and defines the two evaluation modes. Figure 2 varies one data condition at a time and keeps in-weights and in-context accuracy on separate rows. Figure 3 puts the abrupt transition beside pre/post attention maps. Use this empirical sequence when the narrative moves from task to phenomenon to candidate mechanism. [Paper and review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=aN4Jf6Cx69)
- **Meta Continual Learning Revisited** (2024, oral, archived 8/8/10). Table 1 puts prior methods under a shared Hessian-approximation framework. Figure 1 diagrams the iterative update, and Table 3 reports confidence intervals across memory budgets and marks unstable results as omitted. This is a method paper where a comparison table makes the conceptual contribution legible before the workflow figure. [Paper and review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=TpD2aG1h0D)
- **Scaling In-the-Wild Training for Diffusion-based Illumination Harmonization and Editing by Imposing Consistent Light Transport** (2025, oral, archived 10/10/10/10). Figure 1 gives a real object, instructions, and output variants. Figure 2 tracks source data into the training format. Figure 3 contrasts the vanilla and modified objectives, and Figure 4 removes one training element at a time. This separates the capability, the provenance, the mechanism, and the ablation into four distinct visual jobs. [Paper and review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=u1cQYxRI1H)
- **Special Unitary Parameterized Estimators of Rotation** (2026, poster, archived 8/8/8/10). Figure 1 uses a 2D geometric comparison to make an orthogonalization distinction readable, then relates it to 3D projections. Tables 1–2 report precision, timing, and operation counts under stated conditions. Figure 2 exposes performance across error percentiles. The figure explains the mathematical choice while the tables establish its engineering tradeoff. [Paper and review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=VaS6xcDrTb)

- **AlphaEdit** (2025, outstanding paper, archived 8/8/8/8). Figures 1 and 3 show what the original objective balances and where the new edit constrains the update. Figure 2 tracks outcomes over sequential edits. Table 1 separates edit efficacy, generalization, and specificity. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/29c8c615b3187ee995029284702d3f43-Paper-Conference.pdf) · [Review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HvSytvg3Jh)
- **Learning Dynamics of LLM Finetuning** (2025, outstanding paper, archived 6/8/8/10). Figure 2 sketches the direction of probability-mass updates. Figures 3–4 track measured learning dynamics, and Figure 5 compares runs with and without an intervention. The abstract scopes one explanation as hypothetical. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/afe1aa79e5eea7955f553c61a307273e-Paper-Conference.pdf) · [Review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tPNHOoZFl9)
- **LLMs Get Lost In Multi-Turn Conversation** (2026, outstanding paper, archived 6/8/8/10). Figure 1 pairs a worked single-turn/multi-turn contrast with aggregate results. Figures 2–3 explain the simulator flow and comparison conditions. Figure 5 defines and compares the measured quantities. The appendix reports a manual audit of 100 simulations. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/file/59f6421e64707225fdf5b28840679a07-Paper-Conference.pdf) · [Review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=VKGTGGcwl6)
- **Transformers are Inherently Succinct** (2026, outstanding paper, archived 4/8/8/8). The introduction distinguishes which languages can be recognized from how compactly they can be recognized, then gives matching bounds and relevant precision assumptions. The paper uses symbolic constructions and a technical roadmap without conventional numbered figures. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/file/5f7804e8855efe5554025217abc49315-Paper-Conference.pdf) · [Review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Yxz92UuPLQ)

### ICML

- **An Analytic Theory of Creativity in Convolutional Diffusion Models** (2025, oral, archived 4/5/5/5). Figure 2 isolates ideal score matching under different constraints, Figure 3 uses a two-image toy training set to explain the mosaic mechanism, and Figures 5–6 compare theory with trained models. The transition from derivation to prediction lets the reader judge when the explanation works and where attention changes the fit. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/kamb25a.html) · [Review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ilpL2qACla)
- **Probably Approximately Correct Labels** (2026, poster, archived 5/5/6/6). The argument starts with a target labeling error and an annotation budget. Tables 1–2 compare methods under a stated error target, and Figures 1–2 plot realized error and budget savings. This is a guarantee plus measured tradeoff, with each playing a distinct role. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/candes26a.html) · [Review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Yhv4d8wfbi)

- **The Flexibility Trap** (2026, outstanding paper, archived 4/5/5/5). Figure 1 illustrates how decoding orders handle a fork. Figure 3 varies the sampling budget. Figure 5 isolates the degree of order flexibility, and Table 2 distinguishes reproduced baselines. This ties a proposed remedy to the experimentally identified conditions for a failure. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/ni26e.html) · [Review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=kpgURPRMGf)

### NeurIPS

- **Does Reinforcement Learning Really Incentivize Reasoning Capacity in LLMs Beyond the Base Model?** (2025, oral and award runner-up, archived 6/6/6/6). Figure 1 introduces the reasoning-path hypothesis. Figure 2 varies the sampling budget and shows where the comparison changes. Table 1 gives evaluation configurations, and Figures 3–4 extend the comparison to code and visual reasoning. This negative-result paper makes the operating point part of the reader's interpretation. [Paper and review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=4OsgYD7em5) · [Award citation](https://blog.neurips.cc/2025/11/26/announcing-the-neurips-2025-best-paper-awards/)
- **Optimal Mistake Bounds for Transductive Online Learning** (2025, award runner-up, archived 5/5/6/6). Section 1 defines the standard and transductive settings and states the main bound in Theorem 1.1. Figure 1 depicts paths in trees where the lower-bound argument needs them. Use a proof illustration at the proof dependency it clarifies. [Paper and review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=EoebmBe9fG) · [Award citation](https://blog.neurips.cc/2025/11/26/announcing-the-neurips-2025-best-paper-awards/)

- **Entropic Neural Optimal Transport via Diffusion Processes** (2023, oral, archived 7/8/9/9). Figures 1–2 use a deblurring example and a controlled Gaussian-to-mixture example to show intermediate trajectories and effects of regularization. Tables report distinct measurements of transport discrepancy, variability, and image quality. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/eeac51414a11484d048432f614d5bb1b-Paper-Conference.pdf) · [Review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=fHyLsfMDIs)
- **Exploitation of a Latent Mechanism in Graph Contrastive Learning** (2024, oral, archived 8/9/9/9). Figure 1 illustrates possible structure with t-SNE. Figure 2 varies batch normalization. Figure 3 shows the proposed mechanism, followed by task comparisons and ablations. An embedding plot can make a hypothesis visible before an intervention tests it. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/d0ffb35aaa7faa894afe5060c694d674-Paper-Conference.pdf) · [Review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=R8SolCx62K)
- **Why Diffusion Models Don’t Memorize** (2025, best paper, archived 5/5/5/5). Figure 1 introduces qualitative training-time and parameter regimes, and Figures 2–3 show measurements for U-Net models. A separate random-features model carries the theoretical argument. State which setting each diagram or result supports. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/ceb7f3cc876a6dcb15130a645b5a4507-Paper-Conference.pdf) · [Review forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=BSZqpqgqM0)

## Find a current precedent when needed

Start with the target venue's current calls, author instructions, and review form. Retrieve structurally comparable work using the contribution and evidence type along with subject keywords. Where ratings are public, confirm score fields, sort within the same edition and track, and examine high-rated and ordinary accepted examples. Use official proceedings for paper text and official award announcements to identify what a committee recognized. Check figures and their captions in the actual manuscript. Record the problem each figure solves and the claim against which it can be checked. Use the findings as options for the current project rather than a layout mandate.
