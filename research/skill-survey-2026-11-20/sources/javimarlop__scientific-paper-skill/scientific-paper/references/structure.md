# Structure of a paper

Adjust to the journal (its instructions to authors win over this page).

| Section | It answers | Typical content |
|---|---|---|
| Title | What is this about? | The finding or the object, with the key terms; no abbreviations that are not known; no claim the paper does not prove. |
| Abstract | Why read it? | Context, gap, what was done, the main results with numbers, what they mean. One paragraph, within the word limit. |
| Introduction | Why is this question worth asking? | The broad problem; what is known; what is not (the gap); the objective and, in the last paragraph, what the paper does and how it is organised. It ends where the question starts, not with the results. |
| Methods | Could someone repeat it? | Study area or system; data and versions; processing and formulas; analysis and software; validation; where code and data are. |
| Results | What was found? | In the order of the questions, each with its figure or table; numbers with units and uncertainty; no interpretation. |
| Discussion | What does it mean? | The main finding first; comparison with the literature (also what disagrees); mechanisms, flagged as hypotheses when not tested; limitations; implications; next steps. |
| Conclusions | What should the reader keep? | Short; only what the results support; no new data. |
| Back matter | Who and how? | Acknowledgements, funding, author contributions, data and code availability, conflict of interest, AI statement, references. |

## Argument check

Write four sentences and see that the paper keeps them: (1) *We do not know X* (introduction). (2) *We did Y to find it
out* (methods). (3) *We found Z* (results). (4) *So W, with these limits* (discussion). If a paragraph does not serve
one of them, cut it or move it to the supplement.

## Types of document

- **Short communication / letter**: one result; a short introduction; methods compressed (details in the supplement).
- **Review**: the question of the review, how the literature was searched and selected (databases, dates, criteria),
  a synthesis by theme, not paper by paper, and the gaps.
- **Conference paper**: follow the page limit and the template strictly; figures carry more of the load.
